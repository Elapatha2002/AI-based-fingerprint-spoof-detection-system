## What is this study about?

This research evaluates whether AI-generated visual explanations
(heatmaps, attribution maps) of fingerprint spoof-detection decisions are
useful, trustworthy, and appropriate for use in forensic and legal
settings. Your professional judgment will help determine which
explanation methods are most suitable for forensic adoption.

## What will I be asked to do?

You will:

1. Review **three example fingerprint cases**, each showing the system's
   verdict, three different AI explanation methods (Grad-CAM++, SHAP,
   LIME), and the generated forensic report.
2. Answer **32 questions** about the explanations: their clarity,
   trustworthiness, and suitability for forensic reporting and courtroom
   presentation.
3. Provide **optional open-ended comments** about what you would change
   or what is missing.

The survey takes approximately **15–20 minutes**.

## Will my data be kept confidential?

Yes. **No personally identifying information is collected.** Demographic
questions are limited to your professional role, years of experience,
country, and organization *type* (not name). Responses are stored
anonymously and cannot be traced back to you.

## What happens to the data?

- Responses are stored in encrypted form on Microsoft 365 (NSBM
  institutional account) during data collection.
- After analysis, an anonymized aggregate dataset (CSV) and the thesis
  document will be uploaded to a public research repository.
- Open-ended comments may be quoted **verbatim and anonymously** in the
  thesis and any resulting publication. By participating you consent to
  this use.
- Raw response data will be retained for **5 years** after thesis
  submission, then permanently deleted, in accordance with NSBM data
  retention policy.

## Is participation voluntary?

Yes, completely. You may:

- Skip any question
- Withdraw at any time by closing the browser tab — partial responses
  will be discarded
- Request your responses be deleted up to one week after submission by
  emailing the researcher with a self-chosen anonymous code provided at
  the end of the survey

There is no compensation for participation. There is no penalty for
declining.

## Are there any risks?

There are no anticipated risks. The survey does not request operational
details of your work, identifying information about cases, or any
confidential material.
