# Recruitment Kit

Three email templates to use across the recruitment timeline. Personalize
each before sending — generic mass emails are deleted unread.

---

## Email 1 — Recruitment (send NOW, May–June 2026)

**Goal:** Confirm someone is willing to take the survey when it goes live
in August. **Do NOT** send the actual survey link yet.

**Subject:** Forensic AI survey participation — BSc research from NSBM Green University

**Body:**

> Dear *<title + last name>*,
>
> My name is K. M. Pasindu Chanuka Elapatha. I am a final-year BSc (Hons)
> Software Engineering student at NSBM Green University, Sri Lanka. My
> thesis is titled *"Explainable AI-Based Fingerprint Spoof Detection
> System for Digital Forensics."*
>
> A central part of my research evaluates whether AI-generated explanations
> of spoof-detection decisions are useful, trustworthy, and appropriate
> for forensic and legal use. To do this credibly, I need input from
> practitioners with first-hand experience in *<biometrics / digital
> forensics / forensic examination / your area>*.
>
> I am writing to ask whether you would be willing to complete a short,
> anonymous survey (~15 minutes) sometime between **1 and 31 August 2026**.
> The survey will:
>
> - Show three example fingerprint cases analyzed by the system
> - Ask you to rate the clarity, trust, and forensic suitability of three
>   AI explanation methods (Grad-CAM++, SHAP, LIME)
> - Invite open-ended comments
>
> No identifying information is collected. The study has ethics approval
> from the NSBM Faculty of Computing (ref. *<your ref>*). You may withdraw
> at any time. Aggregated results will appear in my thesis and possibly a
> conference paper.
>
> **Would you be willing to participate when the survey opens in August?**
> A simple yes / no reply is enough. If yes, I will email you the survey
> link on August 1, 2026, with a polite reminder on August 15.
>
> I am happy to share an executive summary of the findings once the
> research concludes (November 2026), if useful.
>
> Thank you very much for your time.
>
> Best regards,
> Pasindu Elapatha
> BSc (Hons) Software Engineering · NSBM Green University
> *<email>* · *<phone optional>*
> Supervisor: *<supervisor name + email>*

## Practical recruitment tips

| Channel | How to use |
|---|---|
| **NSBM faculty introductions** | Single most effective channel — ask your supervisor + 2–3 senior faculty for warm introductions to their forensic / biometric contacts |
| **LinkedIn** | Search "digital forensics" + Sri Lanka / India / Bangladesh. Send a short connection note mentioning the study; follow up with full email after they accept |
| **Author lookup of LivDet papers** | Email corresponding authors of recent LivDet papers — many are friendly to undergrad research; cite their work when you reach out |
| **Academic forums** | r/forensics, ResearchGate, ImageJ forums; post a brief recruitment notice (not a survey link) |
| **NSBM alumni** | Alumni in security / banking / telecom often have biometric exposure |
| **In-country bodies** | Sri Lanka CERT, Bar Association of Sri Lanka — for legal-side perspectives |

### Two non-negotiable rules

1. **Never paste the survey URL in Email 1.** Only people who *say yes*
   should receive it in August. Spray-and-pray recruitment gives noisy
   data and a low response rate.
2. **Never offer payment or coursework credit.** That undermines the
   anonymity claim and may invalidate the ethics approval.
